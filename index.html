About

Who I Am

I'm a Mathematics-Statistics graduate from Whitman College, currently working as a Symbolic AI Research Trainee at the Naval Postgraduate School through the Oak Ridge Institute for Science and Education. My work spans formal logic, machine learning, economic statistics, and computational biology.

Originally from Zambia, I bring a cross-cultural perspective to problem-solving. My career interests range from data science and ML/AI engineering to consulting and actuarial science -- any role where I can use quantitative methods to drive meaningful decisions and positive impact.

I believe strongly in making technical knowledge accessible. Whether redesigning survey forms at the Zambia Statistical Agency or translating automated theorem proofs into plain language for military stakeholders, I focus on bridging the gap between rigorous methods and practical understanding.
